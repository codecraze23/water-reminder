Why you'll love it

Reminders with a personality

Everything a reminder app should be — playful on the surface, serious about never letting you forget.

🕷️

Swinging Animations

Physics-inspired pendulum swing in & out, with adjustable speed from a relaxed 0.5× to a zippy 2.0×. Smooth, bouncy, alive.

🎨

Dark Glass UI

A rounded 20px glass card with a soft border glow, a live typewriter message and a real delivery timestamp. Gorgeous, never jarring.

🕒

Flexible Scheduling

Unlimited custom reminders — repeat every N minutes, or fire at fixed times like 09:00, 13:30 and 18:00. Water, breaks, medicine, anything.

🎭

Your Own Characters

Drop any transparent PNG, GIF or WebP into the characters folder — Spider-Man, an anime avatar, your pet. Built-in emoji heroes included.

🔕

Snooze & Do Not Disturb

One-click 5-minute snooze, instant dismiss, and a global Do Not Disturb toggle right from the system tray for deep-focus hours.

🖱️

Truly Non-Intrusive

The overlay is click-through — your work never gets blocked. Auto-starts with Windows, and settings live safely in your AppData.
